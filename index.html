Why NATI

Elevating the Standards of Aviation.

The Nethawk Aviation Training Institute (NATI) trains the people behind safe airspace — air traffic communicators, aerodrome rescue and firefighting personnel, and aviation executives.

Our graduates leave with more than a certificate: the procedures, discipline, and judgement to perform under real operational pressure.
