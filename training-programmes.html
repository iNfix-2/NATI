School 01 — 5 Courses

Air Traffic Services / Communication

Communication, navigation, and information management courses for ATS personnel, built to ICAO standards.

01
ATS & COMMUNICATION

English Language Proficiency Test

ICAO Level 4 Short Course

Course overview — The aim of the course is to prepare participants in English language used in aviation so as to communicate effectively within the aviation community on at least operational level four (4) of the ICAO Language Proficiency requirement rating scale. Students should be able to aspire for higher levels.

02
ATS & COMMUNICATION

Aeronautical Mobile Service Course (AMS)

ICAO Standards Short Course

Course overview — The aim of the Aeronautical Mobile Service course (AMS) is to provide training that will produce highly skilled and advanced personnel that will serve as providers in the exchange of communication operations necessary for ensuring safety of air navigation in the aviation industry in accordance with ICAO standards.

03
ATS & COMMUNICATION

Terminal / Area ATC Radar Refresher Course (ATCCRR)

Radar Operations Refresher

Course overview — To enable participants to provide air traffic control services, flight information service and aerodrome information service to air traffic, based on the use of radar.

04
ATS & COMMUNICATION

Advanced Aeronautical Information Management Officers Course

AAIMO Advanced

Course overview — The aim of the Advanced Aeronautical Information Management (AAIMO) course is to provide advanced training that will produce highly skilled personnel that will ensure the flow (collection and dissemination), and management of aeronautical information necessary for safety and regularity of air navigation in accordance with ICAO standards.

05
ATS & COMMUNICATION

Air Traffic Services / Communication — Foundation Course

Foundation General Aviation

Course overview — To introduce participants to the general principles and practices in the aviation industry. To familiarize participants with air traffic services (ATS) procedures, airspace management and air traffic flow management, especially in general aviation operations.

School 02 — 3 Courses

Airport Emergency Training

Rescue, firefighting, and emergency response courses for aerodrome personnel — from entry level to fire officers.

06
EMERGENCY & FIRE

Airport Emergency Procedure (AEP)

Emergency Response Aerodrome

Course overview — This course is aimed at enhancing the capability, effectiveness and efficiency of emergency response personnel in handling occurrences at an aerodrome or its vicinity.

07
EMERGENCY & FIRE

Competent Aircraft Live Fire Simulator

Fire Officers Tactical Command

Course overview — To equip experienced airport firefighters with the theory, principles and practice of fire station management, and the skill required to perform tactical firefighting involving various emergencies and fire-ground command, to prepare them for their roles as airport fire officers.

08
EMERGENCY & FIRE

Initial Fire Fighting Course (IFF)

Entry Level Rescue & Firefighting

Course overview — To enable entry level personnel to identify and interpret theories, facts, concepts, principles, procedures, equipment, and components of aerodrome rescue and firefighting. At the end of the course, trainees will demonstrate the knowledge and skill required to perform basic rescue and firefighting services for aircraft operations.

School 03 — 1 Course

Executive & Management

Decision-level training for senior executives overseeing aviation planning and operations.

09
EXECUTIVE & MANAGEMENT

Executive Aviation Planning, Operations and Management Course

Senior Executives Planning & Operations

Course overview — To provide senior executives with a comprehensive understanding of aviation planning, operations and management, enabling them to make informed decisions related to aviation activities and achieve business objectives.
